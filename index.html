10-minute rapid review

  • Aerobic = O2, lots of ATP (~36–38), CO2 + water. Anaerobic = 2 ATP + lactic acid → acidosis → Na/K pump fails → cell swells and dies.
  • Perfusion needs pump + container + fluid. Shock = hypoperfusion.
  • Air: N2 78%, O2 21%, CO2 0.04%.
  • Boyle: chest volume up → pressure down → air in. Exhalation at rest is passive.
  • Minute volume = VT × RR. Alveolar ventilation = (VT − 150) × RR. Fast + shallow = poor alveolar ventilation.
  • Drive: CO2 (medulla) first; low O2 (carotid/aortic bodies) is the backup.
  • V/Q: no blood flow = dead space (PE); no air = shunt (pneumonia).
  • O2 ~98% on hemoglobin. CO2 ~70% as bicarbonate.
  • Hydrostatic pushes out; oncotic (albumin) pulls in; leaky capillaries = edema.
  • CO = HR × SV. BP = CO × SVR. SV = preload, afterload, contractility. Frank–Starling = stretch → stronger squeeze.
  • Baroreceptors (carotid sinus, aortic arch) → low BP → tachycardia, vasoconstriction, pale cool skin. Low BP is a LATE sign.
  • Receptors: alpha-1 vessels constrict; beta-1 heart faster/stronger; beta-2 lungs dilate.
  • Blood path: RA → tricuspid → RV → pulmonic → lungs → LA → mitral → LV → aortic → aorta.
  • Adequate breathing = adequate rate AND depth.

Class 11/16 — must-memorize ★

  • Alpha-1 vasoconstriction · beta-1 heart rate and force · beta-2 bronchodilation.
  • Agonist activates a receptor; antagonist blocks it.
  • Skin: epidermis → dermis → subcutaneous.
  • Valves TPMA: tricuspid, pulmonic, mitral, aortic.
  • Conduction: SA → AV → bundle of His → bundle branches → Purkinje. ECG: P atria, QRS ventricles, T recovery.
  • Upper/lower airway divide at the cricoid cartilage.
  • Spine: C1–C7, T1–T12, L1–L5, sacrum 5 fused, coccyx ~4 fused.
  • Terms: hypovolemic, hyperglycemia, apnea, tachycardia, bradypnea (prefix + root + suffix).
