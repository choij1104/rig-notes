# Rig Notes — EMT 26F study app

Personal study app for Texas 26 F EMT I (National EMS Academy San Antonio).

- `index.html` — Chapters (default): each chapter opens with a one-minute summary, then that chapter's own read-aloud audio (play/pause/resume, section skip, speed), then the full notes, then its cards and quiz. Also due dates, flashcards, practice quiz, listen mode, rapid review.
- `audio/` — MP3s for listening while driving (Module 1).

Chapters covered: Ch 1–14 and 37–39 (Prehospital Emergency Care, 12th ed.), grouped by course module.
All notes, cards and questions are written in original wording. Every card and quiz item carries a chapter tag (item order unchanged, so saved progress is kept).
